DEWAEcoApp

Designed during the DIDI Design Competition (Dec. 2024–Jun. 2025), DEWAEcoApp explores how people can understand household utility use, compare water-saving products, and earn rewards for verified sustainable choices. Building on the original React product calculator and a separate Bolt utility-tracker prototype, I developed a unified, public interactive preview.

Try the interactive app ↗Published app sourceOriginal React calculator

Independent student project, not affiliated with Dubai Electricity & Water Authority. The live preview can display hypothetical conservation points but does not award official or redeemable DEWA points, authenticate utility documents, or access customer shopping accounts.

What you can try

Real interactions, explicit prototype boundaries.

01 / DASHBOARD

Utility tracking

Start by photographing or uploading a utility bill. After reviewing extracted values, the app tracks consumption, compares consecutive billing months, and demonstrates hypothetical conservation points when water or electricity usage decreases. Points are not issued by DEWA or redeemable.

02 / ECO PRODUCTS

Side-by-side recommendations

Explore explicitly illustrative product examples by category, compare estimated water use and five-year cost, and open Amazon UAE or Carrefour UAE searches. Products are not live retailer listings.

03 / WATER CALCULATOR

Adjustable product scenarios

Add products or transfer one from Eco Products. Calculate volume and variable water costs using category-aware litres/minute, litres/flush, or litres/cycle measurements and editable usage assumptions.

04 / DOCUMENT REVIEW

Photo and receipt organization

Upload an image, attempt browser-based OCR, review extracted data, and save a local unverified review record. Duplicate checks are device-local; actual identity or purchase verification needs authorized services.

Design rationale

DEWAEcoApp began with two complementary tracks: reward sustainable product purchases and reward measurable reductions in water or electricity usage. The live application demonstrates the monthly comparison: two consecutive photo-imported bills with the same entered account number are compared by water (m³) and electricity (kWh), not just the amount paid. In the current illustrative scoring rule, a decrease in water consumption gives +10 simulated points and a decrease in electricity consumption gives another +10. Unchanged or increased consumption earns none. The product calculator explored shopping choices, while the utility-tracker prototype explored household conservation. The demo points are a proposed incentive only, not an authorized DEWA rewards program.

The sample product catalog deliberately avoids claims of product-level certification. The water estimator uses an editable AED-per-cubic-metre assumption; real DEWA tariffs apply slab rates, fuel surcharges, VAT, and other bill items, so the displayed model is for comparison rather than bill prediction.

Implementation & constraints

Original source

React, TypeScript, Tailwind and Lucide. Typed product records; state-based product editing; manual monthly, annual, and five-year comparisons; mocked purchase and account-linking flows.

Inspect React source ↗

Live standalone preview

A build-free HTML/CSS/JavaScript release with local persistence, accessible input forms, unit-aware calculations, responsive cards, and on-device image recognition when its external OCR library is available.

Inspect deployed source ↗

True customer accounts, encrypted backend evidence storage, anti-fraud verification across users, retailer order-history access, and bill-credit redemption require additional infrastructure and explicit approval from participating providers. No client-side prototype can truthfully substitute for these controls.
