Scan & review documents

Upload a photo of a bill or receipt, extract text, and save an organized review record.

Important: OCR reads characters; it does not prove that a bill or receipt is authentic. This application has no official retailer or utility verification. Consecutive photographed bills for the same entered account may generate simulated points only (+10 if water usage drops; +10 if electricity usage drops). No redeemable points can be earned. Do not upload sensitive real customer documents to a shared device.
