Today

What is happening today showcasing the tragic effects of climate change?

California

Trees sway in the fire - Ethan Swope, Source: AP News (License: Creative Commons License)

California has experienced years of wildfires, yet the frequency and severity have increased. California ranks among the most vulnerable states to climate change. Being on the West Coast, it is prone to high-temperature increases, a main driver of wildfires. Reports state over 80,000 residents have been displaced, 16,000+ homes destroyed, and 29 casualties in January.

Maldives

Maldives Island - Source: Rawlevels.com (License: Creative Commons License)

Due to the Maldives being an island, it is very vulnerable to climate change impacts. The land is only 1.5 meters above sea level. Rising sea levels have led to land destruction. Coastal erosion has occurred due to rising sea levels and wave activity, causing a loss of beaches, destruction of infrastructure, and more seawater entering inland areas.
