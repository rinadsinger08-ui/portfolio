Taking Action Against Climate Change

Biking - Stefan Haehnel, Source: Flickr (License: Creative Common License)

Invest in a bike for shorter distances. Not only is it great exercise, it also reduces CO2 emissions from cars!

Dubai Metro in Dubai - Tim Adams, Source: Own Work (License: Creative Commons License)

Get yourself a metro for quick and more sustainable transportation. The less people out with cars at the same time, the better.

Rooftop Solutions - Source: Rawpixel.com (License: Creative Commons License)

Look into renewable energy such as solar panels, which are the cleaner and cheaper option.

LED Lights - Meckbach, Source: The CSR Journal (License: Creative Commons License)

Be mindful of your household's energy use. Turn off lights and appliances when not needed. Consider switching to LED lights which reduce carbon footprint up to a third!

Training on how to reduce water consumption - Source: The Claire Herald (License: Creative Commons License)

Reduce water consumption with shorter showers, using cold start functions or backends on sinks, putting a bucket under shower to collect excess water for gardening.

Reduce, Recycle, Reuse - Source: Free Malaysia Today (License: Creative Commons License)

Recycle, Reduce, Reuse. Start by adding a recycling bin to your home, buying a reusable water bottle, keeping and reusing shopping bags, etc.
