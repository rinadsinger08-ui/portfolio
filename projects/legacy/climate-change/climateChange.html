What is climate change?

Climate Change - Source: Needpix.com (License: Common Creative License)

Climate change is the long term changes in temperature and weather patterns.

A coal fired power plant on the Ohio River just west of Cincinnati - Robert S. Donovan, Source: The Paw Print (License: Creative Commons License)

The main driver of this phenomena is the burning of fossil fuels which releases CO2, a greenhouse gas that facilitates climate change through trapping the sun's heat.

NEC4 X29 Climate Change - Glenn Hide, Source: GMH Planning (License: Creative Common License)

As a result, our Earth has been heating, sea levels have been rising, and ecosystems are endangered.
