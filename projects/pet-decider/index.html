How matching works

Space, daily time, both budgets, housing permission, backup care, and your furry-animal preference are required constraints. Activity alignment adds two ranking points; compatible experience adds one. Ties sort by pet ID. Ranking points are not a suitability probability.

Numeric thresholds are editable assumptions for this demo. They do not determine whether an individual animal will thrive. Read the linked care guides and discuss your situation with a shelter before adopting.
